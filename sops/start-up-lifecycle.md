# Start-Up Lifecycle
Type: Timeline

How a start-up job runs, from the first request to the warranty handoff. This is general information that won't cover every scenario. Contact your Team Leader for clarification as needed.

## Overview of the Process

### Kickoff
- The project management (PM) team submits a request for start-up to the Superintendent.
- The PM team hosts a kickoff meeting to discuss scope of work and provide access to submittals, vendors, BOD, SOO, schedule, commissioning plan, prints, and site requirements.
- Start-up techs collaborate with the PM team to create project checklists, forms, and documentation (for example, CxA forms, pre-functional test scripts, and sequence validation scripts).

### Walkdown and Readiness
- The lead tech walks the entire project, confirms systems with markup prints, and highlights time-sensitive issues with the PX, PM, or QA-QC.
- Technicians work with wet, dry, electrical, controls, and the GC to verify equipment is ready for startup.
- The Start-Up team visually verifies overall system readiness.

### Start-Up
- Trades signoff is completed (verify valves, dampers, utilities, and so on).
- Technicians perform final startup.
- Technicians support vendor startup and provide documentation to the PM team.

### Turnover and Commissioning
- Technicians complete any documentation required for "turnover" of systems to the air and water balance and controls contractors.
- Technicians aid in the commissioning effort as directed by the PM team.
- Technicians complete all punch list items.

### Training and Warranty
- Technicians perform customer training.
- Technicians assist the Superintendent and PM team in setting up warranty protocol.
- Technicians collaborate with the Start-Up Superintendent and Service Department to provide a PM contract (if applicable).
