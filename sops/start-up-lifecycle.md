## Overview of the Process

1. The project management (PM) team submits a request for start-up.
2. The PM team hosts a kickoff meeting to discuss scope of work and provide access to submittals, vendors, BOD, SOO, schedule, commissioning plan, prints, and site requirements.
3. Start-up techs collaborate with the PM team to create project checklists, forms, and documentation.
4. The lead tech walks the entire project, confirms systems with markup prints, and highlights time-sensitive issues.
5. Technicians work with wet/dry/electrical/controls/GC to verify equipment is ready for startup.
6. Start-Up team visually verifies all systems.
7. Trades signoff is completed (verify valves, dampers, utilities, etc.).
8. Technicians perform final startup.
9. Technicians support vendor startup and provide documentation to the PM team.
10. Technicians complete any documentation required for "turnover" of systems to the air/water balance and controls contractors.
11. Technicians aid in the commissioning effort as directed by the PM team.
12. Technicians complete all punch list items.
13. Technicians perform customer training.
14. Technicians assist the Superintendent/PM team in setting up warranty protocol.
15. Technicians collaborate with the Start-Up Superintendent and Service Department to provide a PM contract (if applicable).
