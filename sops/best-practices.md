# Best Practices

Day-to-day tasks and standard practices for all field personnel.

<details class="accordion">
<summary>General Notes</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

<ul class="chunked-list">
<li>**"Pass down" to the Project Lead:** completed goals, outstanding issues/problems, and/or deficiencies **prior** to emailing clients, the General Contractor, or the PM team.</li>
<li>**Lead** tech should **"CC"** Start-Up Superintendent on **all** email correspondence.</li>
<li>Lead techn supplies **weekly written updates** to the appropriate Start-Up Superintendent.</li>
<li>Notify the Start-Up Superintendent of any workforce changes or concerns.</li>
<li>Wear clean uniform shirts (service calls) or SI t-shirts.</li>
<li>Contact IT Helpdesk before reaching out to the office.</li>
<li>Turn in timecards **before 8:00 AM on Friday** (weekend adjustments are made on Monday morning).</li>
<li>**Time off requests** go through the Start-Up Superintendent by email.</li>
</ul>

</div>
</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>Professionalism</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

<ul class="chunked-list">
<li>Show up ready to work with all the necessary tools.</li>
<li>Be on time to the job site and leave at the appropriate time. The typical shift is **6:00 AM to 2:30 PM**.</li>
<li>Avoid confrontations while on-site.</li>
<li>Be professional in all email communications.</li>
<li>Be part of the solution, not the problem.</li>
<li>Always conduct yourself as if there is a camera on you.</li>
<li>Keep your van looking clean (including the dash).</li>
<li>Be prompt at all site meetings, look engaged, but not overbearing.</li>
<li>Fill out documentation appropriately; do not add unprofessional comments or verbiage.</li>
<li>Watch your language while on a job site.</li>
</ul>

</div>
</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>Dispatch</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

<p><em>UC & Sac offices are the 50-mile reference points.</em></p>

<ul class="chunked-list">
<li>**Do not leave** your current job site for another site without informing the Start-Up Superintendent via email, phone, or text.</li>
<li>**Do not dispatch** yourself or others without discussing it with the Start-Up Superintendent (phone, text, or email).</li>
<li>**ALL overtime** beyond two hours per day must be approved by the Start-Up Superintendent before committing to the work request.</li>
<li>**When working weekends**, lead tech provides EOD status to the appropriate Superintendent before leaving the job site ("project complete" or "return needed", employee general welfare, and additional workforce issues).</li>
<li>When contacted directly by Project Management requesting overtime or weekend support: 
    > Get scope details, and tell the requestor you will let them know workforce availability **AFTER** you discuss the request and scope with the appropriate Start-Up Superintendent.</li>
<li>Every technician is responsible for checking in and out of the various Union Halls and notifying the UC office (Monica C.) of any changes.</li>
<li>**Do not call** any techs for OT work on your projects without discussing it with the Start-Up Superintendent (priority is given to those low on hours or seniority).</li>
</ul>

</div>
</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>Vehicles</summary>
<div class="accordion-body">

<ul class="chunked-list">
<li>All vehicle accidents must be reported to UC Safety and Corp Safety **at once**, with follow-up to the Start-Up Superintendent.</li>
<li>Keep your vehicle clean, inside and out.</li>
<li>You are responsible for **vehicle maintenance** and keeping all safety gear inventoried/updated.</li>
<li>Contact Derrick Brice for spare vehicles and return them cleaned.</li>
<li>Coordinate with Derrick Brice about repairs; you are responsible for following up on your repairs.</li>
<li>Track all van tools on your **inventory sheet** — the Start-Up Superintendent will request a report as needed.</li>
<li>Monthly online driving assignments must be completed **before the 15th**.</li>
<li>Technicians are responsible for all vehicle tickets (contact Area GF).</li>
</ul>

</div>
</details>

<div class="accordion-body">
