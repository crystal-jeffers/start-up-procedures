<h1>Best Practices</h1>
Day-to-day tasks and standard practices for all field personnel.


<details class="accordion"> <summary>General Notes</summary> <div class="accordion-body">
<ul class="chunked-list"> <li><b>"Pass down" to the Project Lead</b>: completed goals, outstanding issues/problems and/or deficiencies <b>prior to emailing clients</b>, the General Contractor, or the PM team.</li> <li>"CC" Start-Up Superintendent on all email correspondence.</li> <li>Lead tech supplies <b>weekly written updates</b> to their Start-Up Superintendent.</li> <li>Notify the Start-Up Superintendent of any workforce changes or concerns.</li> <li>Wear clean SI uniform shirts on service calls.</li> <li>Contact IT Helpdesk to resolve technical issues.</li> <li>Turn in timecards <b>before 8:00 AM on Friday</b> (weekend adjustments made on Monday morning).</li> <li><b>Time off requests</b> go through the Start-Up Superintendent
by email.</li> </ul>
</div> </details>
<details class="accordion"> <summary>Professionalism</summary> <div class="accordion-body">
<ul class="chunked-list"> <li>Show up ready to work with all the necessary tools.</li> <li>Be on time to the job site and leave at the appropriate time. The typical shift is <b>6:00 AM to 2:30 PM</b>.</li> <li>Avoid confrontation while on-site.</li> <li>Any emails outside the team should be professional.</li> <li>Be part of the solution, not the problem.</li> <li>Always conduct yourself as if there is a camera on you.</li> <li>Keep your van looking clean (including the dash).</li> <li>Be prompt at all site meetings, look engaged, but not overbearing.</li> <li>Fill out documentation appropriately; do not add unprofessional comments or verbiage.</li> <li>Watch your language while on a job site.</li> </ul>
</div> </details>
<details class="accordion"> <summary>Dispatch</summary> <div class="accordion-body">
<p><em>UC & Sac offices are the 50-mile reference points.</em></p>
<ul class="chunked-list"> <li><b>Do not leave</b> your current job site for another site without informing the Start-Up Superintendent via email, phone, or text.</li> <li><b>Do not dispatch</b> yourself or others without discussing it with the Start-Up Superintendent (phone, text, or email).</li> <li><b>ALL overtime</b> beyond two hours per day is to be discussed with the Start-Up Superintendent <b>before</b> committing to the work request.</li> <li>When working weekends, lead technician provides <b>EOD status</b> to the appropriate Superintendent before leaving the job site ("project completed" or "return needed", employee general welfare, and additional workforce issues).</li> <li><b>When contacted directly by Project Management</b> requesting overtime or weekend support: get scope details, and tell the requestor you will let them know workforce availability AFTER you discuss the request and scope with the appropriate Start-Up Superintendent.</li> <li>Every technician is responsible for checking in and out of the various Union Halls and notifying the UC office (Monica C.) of any changes.</li> <li><b>Do not call</b> any technicians for OT work on your projects without discussing it with the Start-Up Superintendent (priority is given to those low on hours or seniority).</li>
</ul>
</div> </details>
<details class="accordion"> <summary>Tools</summary> <div class="accordion-body">
<p><em>New site setup pending.</em></p>
<ul class="chunked-list"> <li>All tools or equipment <b>purchases over $200</b> must be approved by the Start-Up
Superintendent <b>before</b> purchase.</li> <li><b>Start-Up Cage</b> (new sites pending): when checking tools back into the cage, ensure they are clean and charged for the next technician.</li> <li>The cage is for <b>tools only</b>, not supplies.</li>
<li>Tools must returned be in the same condition or better than when they left the cage.</li> <li>If tools have issues, inform the Start-Up Superintendent and note them on the <b>checkout form</b>.</li> <li><b>Tool Cage Check Out Form</b>: link to be added.</li> </ul>
</div> </details>
<details class="accordion"> <summary>Vehicles</summary> <div class="accordion-body">
<ul class="chunked-list"> <li><b>All vehicle accidents</b> must be reported to UC Safety and Corp Safety <b>at once</b>, with follow-up to the Start-Up Superintendent.</li> <li>Keep your vehicle clean, inside and out.</li> <li>You are responsible for vehicle maintenance and keeping all safety gear inventoried/updated.</li> <li>Contact Derrick Brice for spare vehicles and return them cleaned.</li> <li>Coordinate with Derrick Brice about repairs; you are responsible for following up on your repairs.</li> <li>Track all van tools on your <b>inventory sheet</b> — the Start-Up Superintendent will request a report as needed.</li> <li>Monthly online driving assignments must be completed <b>before the 15th</b>.</li>
<li>Technicians are responsible for all vehicle tickets (contact Area GF).</li> </ul>
</div> </details>
<details class="accordion"> <summary>Safety</summary> <div class="accordion-body">
<ul class="chunked-list"> <li><b>All injuries</b> must be reported to site safety, project lead tech, Area GF, UC Safety, and the Start-Up Superintendent.</li> <li><b>PPE</b>: Each technician is responsible for using the correct PPE for any task. Site Safety, the lead technician, or the Start-Up Superintendent can assist if needed.</li> <li>Pre-task planning, safety tailgates, and
defensive driving must be completed as required.</li> </ul>
</div> </details>
