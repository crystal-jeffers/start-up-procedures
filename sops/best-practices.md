# Best Practices
Type: Reference

Day-to-day tasks and standard practices for all field personnel.

## Numbers to know
- **6:00 AM to 2:30 PM** The typical shift.
- **Friday, 8:00 AM** Timecards due. Weekend adjustments are made Monday morning.
- **2 hours** Overtime past this, per day, is discussed with the Start-Up Superintendent first.
- **$200** Tool or equipment purchases over this need approval before you buy.
- **The 15th** Monthly online driving assignments are due before this date.
- **50 miles** Measured from the UC and Sac offices for dispatch.

## General Notes
- **"Pass down" to the Project Lead**: completed goals, outstanding issues or problems, and deficiencies **before emailing clients**, the General Contractor, or the PM team.
- "CC" the Start-Up Superintendent on all email correspondence.
- The lead tech supplies **weekly written updates** to their Start-Up Superintendent.
- Notify the Start-Up Superintendent of any workforce changes or concerns.
- Wear clean SI uniform shirts on service calls.
- Contact the IT Helpdesk to resolve technical issues.
- Turn in timecards **before 8:00 AM on Friday** (weekend adjustments are made Monday morning).
- **Time off requests** go through the Start-Up Superintendent by email.

## Professionalism
- Show up ready to work with all the necessary tools.
- Be on time to the job site and leave at the appropriate time. The typical shift is **6:00 AM to 2:30 PM**.
- Avoid confrontation while on-site.
- Keep any emails outside the team professional.
- Be part of the solution, not the problem.
- Always conduct yourself as if there is a camera on you.
- Keep your van looking clean (including the dash).
- Be prompt at all site meetings. Look engaged, but not overbearing.
- Fill out documentation appropriately. Don't add unprofessional comments or wording.
- Watch your language while on a job site.

## Dispatch
*UC and Sac offices are the 50-mile reference points.*

- **Do not leave** your current job site for another site without informing the Start-Up Superintendent by email, phone, or text.
- **Do not dispatch** yourself or others without discussing it with the Start-Up Superintendent (phone, text, or email).
- **All overtime** beyond two hours per day is discussed with the Start-Up Superintendent **before** committing to the work request.
- When working weekends, the lead technician provides an **EOD status** to the appropriate Superintendent before leaving the job site ("project completed" or "return needed", employee general welfare, and any additional workforce issues).
- **When Project Management contacts you directly** to request overtime or weekend support, get the scope details. Then tell the requestor you'll confirm workforce availability AFTER you discuss the request and scope with the appropriate Start-Up Superintendent.
- Every technician is responsible for checking in and out of the various Union Halls and notifying the UC office of any changes.
- **Do not call** any technicians for OT work on your projects without discussing it with the Start-Up Superintendent (priority goes to those low on hours or seniority).

## Tools
*New site setup pending.*

- All tool or equipment **purchases over $200** must be approved by the Start-Up Superintendent **before** purchase.
- **Start-Up Cage** (new sites pending): when checking tools back into the cage, make sure they're clean and charged for the next technician.
- The cage is for **tools only**, not supplies.
- Return tools in the same condition or better than when they left the cage.
- If tools have issues, tell the Start-Up Superintendent and note them on the **checkout form**.
- **Tool Cage Check Out Form**: [open the form](#add-checkout-form-link).

## Vehicles
- Report **all vehicle accidents** to UC Safety and Corp Safety **at once**, with follow-up to the Start-Up Superintendent.
- Keep your vehicle clean, inside and out.
- You're responsible for vehicle maintenance and keeping all safety gear inventoried and up to date.
- Contact the fleet coordinator for spare vehicles, and return them clean.
- Coordinate repairs with the fleet coordinator. You're responsible for following up on your repairs.
- Track all van tools on your **inventory sheet**. The Start-Up Superintendent will request a report as needed.
- Complete monthly online driving assignments **before the 15th**.
- Technicians are responsible for all vehicle tickets (contact the Area GF).

## Safety
- Report **all injuries** to site safety, the project lead tech, the Area GF, UC Safety, and the Start-Up Superintendent.
- **PPE**: each technician is responsible for using the correct PPE for any task. Site Safety, the lead technician, or the Start-Up Superintendent can help if needed.
- Complete pre-task planning, safety tailgates, and defensive driving as required.
