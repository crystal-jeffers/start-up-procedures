# Best Practices

Day-to-day tasks and standard practices for all field personnel.

## General Notes

- "Pass down" to the Project Lead: completed goals, outstanding issues/problems, and/or deficiencies prior to emailing clients, the General Contractor, or the PM team.
- Lead tech should "CC" Start-Up Superintendent on all email correspondence.
- The lead technician supplies weekly written updates to the appropriate Start-Up Superintendent.
- Please notify the Start-Up Superintendent of any workforce changes or concerns.
- All technicians should wear clean uniform shirts (service calls) or SI t-shirts.
- Please contact the IT "desk help" before reaching out to Norm in the office.
- Please turn in your TC before 8:00 AM on Friday (weekend adjustments made on Monday morning).
- Time off requests go through the Start-Up Superintendent by email.

## Professionalism

- Show up ready to work with all the necessary tools.
- Be on time to the job site and leave at the appropriate time. The typical shift is 6:00 AM to 2:30 PM.
- Try to avoid any confrontations while on-site.
- Any emails outside of the team should be professional — [tips on writing professional emails](https://www.rit.edu/behindthebricks/content/10-tips-writing-professional-emails).
- Try to be part of the solution and not the problem.
- Always conduct yourself as if there is a camera on you.
- Keep your van looking clean (including the dash).
- Be prompt at all site meetings, look engaged, but not overbearing.
- Fill out documentation appropriately; do not add unprofessional comments or verbiage.
- Watch your language while on a job site.

## Dispatch

*(UC & Sac offices are the 50-mile reference points.)*

- Do not leave your current job site for another site without informing the Start-Up Superintendent via email, phone, or text.
- Do not dispatch yourself or others without discussing it with the Start-Up Superintendent (phone, text, or email).
- ALL overtime beyond two hours per day is to be discussed with the Start-Up Superintendent before committing to the work request.
- When working weekends, the lead technician provides EOD status to the appropriate Superintendent before leaving the job site (project completed or need to return tomorrow, employee general welfare, and additional workforce issues).
- When contacted directly by Project Management requesting overtime or weekend support: get scope details, and tell the requestor you will let them know workforce availability AFTER you discuss the request and scope with the appropriate Start-Up Superintendent.
- Every technician is responsible for checking in and out of the various Union Halls and notifying the UC office (Monica C.) of any changes.
- Please do not call any technicians for OT work on your projects without discussing it with the Start-Up Superintendent (priority is given to those low on hours or seniority).

## Vehicles

- All vehicle accidents must be reported to UC Safety and Corp Safety at once, with follow-up to the Start-Up Superintendent.
- Please keep your vehicle clean inside and out.
- You are responsible for vehicle maintenance and keeping all safety gear inventoried/updated.
- Contact Derrick Brice for spare vehicles and return them cleaned.
- Coordinate with Derrick Brice about repairs; you are responsible for following up on your repairs.
- Track all van tools on your inventory sheet — the Start-Up Superintendent will request a report as needed.
- Monthly online driving assignments must be completed before the 15th.
- Technicians are responsible for all vehicle tickets (contact Area GF).

## Safety

- All injuries must be reported to site safety, project lead tech, Area GF, UC Safety, and the Start-Up Superintendent.
- PPE: each technician is responsible for using the correct PPE for any task. Site Safety, the lead technician, or the Start-Up Superintendent can assist if needed.
- Pre-task planning, safety tailgates, and defensive driving must be completed as required.
