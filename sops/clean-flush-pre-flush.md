# Pre-Flush
Type: Form

Get the system ready before any water moves, and calculate its volume for the cleaning and treatment stages.

<details class="accordion"> <summary>How to do this stage</summary> <div class="accordion-body">
The flushing procedure is designed for carbon steel and copper distribution systems only. It removes most of the weld slag, scale, dirt, and rust in the system. Each system's normal circulation pumps circulate the cleaning solution.

1. Remove and document any instrumentation that may be damaged by the flushing procedure. Close off valves to differential pressure transmitters not required to run the system, close off valves to expansion tanks, remove flow meters, and close valves that connect to pro-press copper lines.
2. Verify new system pumps have been energized, rotation is correct, and they are functional for this procedure.
3. Verify construction strainers and gaskets are available for the suction diffusers. Provide a fine mesh at the existing suction diffusers to catch any debris.
4. Calculate the approximate volume of the system. This value sets the amounts needed for cleaning and treatment.
</div> </details>
