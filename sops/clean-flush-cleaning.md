# Cleaning
Type: Form

Circulate cleaner through each loop for at least 12 hours, test the solution, then bleed and feed until the system water matches the make-up water.

<details class="accordion"> <summary>How to do this stage</summary> <div class="accordion-body">
This procedure removes oil, grease, dirt, and other contaminants. Afterward, the system water's TDS is compared to the make-up water's. Matching levels confirm all cleaner has been removed. The system pH is checked before draining.

### Before cleaning
1. Read the SDS for the cleaning chemical. Make sure all required PPE is ready before handling any cleaning chemicals.
2. Verify the concentration required for the cleaning agent, and the amount required based on the system volume.
3. Verify the system is full, flushing is complete, and the pumps are still running and circulating.

### Cleaning
4. Based on the amount of cleaning agent required, bleed off that amount from the full system.
5. Add the cleaner using appropriate safety measures.
6. Circulate for a minimum of 1 hour with ALL valves open, so the chemical fully mixes through the loop.
7. Line up the system and circulate for a minimum of 12 hours through all HHW pipe in the building, including boxes, coils, and equipment. Only items removed for this procedure are left out.

### Testing and bleed and feed
8. Test the system water and the make-up water for total dissolved solids. Test the system pH. Water discharged must be under pH 11. If it isn't, dilute it with water to reach pH 11 before discharging to the sanitary system.
9. Document and report the values for acceptance by the CxA.
10. Once the results are accepted, start the bleed and feed right away. Open all valves to circulate the whole system, and bleed and feed until the TDS reading matches the make-up water.
</div> </details>

> Warning: Read the SDS and have all required PPE on hand before handling any cleaning chemicals.

## Bleed and feed
- [ ] Document and report the values for acceptance by the IOR or commissioning agent before discharging to drain.
- [ ] Start the system bleed and feed with all valves open to flush the cleaner out of the entire system.
- [ ] Blow down all low point drains and air separators to remove any debris deposited during the flush and clean.
- [ ] Stop the bleed and feed when the TDS reading matches the make-up water TDS.
- [ ] Check 10% of the strainers at coil kits, in different parts of the building, for debris. If any are clogged, all strainers must be cleaned.
