# Treatment
Type: Form

Charge the cleaned and flushed system with corrosion inhibitor, then confirm the concentration with a lab sample.

<details class="accordion"> <summary>How to do this stage</summary> <div class="accordion-body">
This procedure is for systems that have already been cleaned and flushed. It adds a corrosion inhibitor to the system.

1. Read the SDS for the corrosion inhibitor. Make sure all required PPE is ready before handling any treatment chemicals.
2. Confirm the system TDS reading matches the make-up water TDS.
3. Charge the system with the approved corrosion inhibitor to the desired concentration.
4. The water tech verifies the concentration by field testing and sending a sample to the testing lab.
</div> </details>

> Warning: Corrosion inhibitors are usually a strong base (high pH). Follow every safety measure, and have all required PPE on hand.
