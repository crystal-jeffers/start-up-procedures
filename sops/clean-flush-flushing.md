# Flushing
Type: Form

Fill the system with ICW and bleed and feed for at least 4 hours, until the water runs clear.

<details class="accordion"> <summary>How to do this stage</summary> <div class="accordion-body">
1. Make sure all heating hot water supply and return valves at VAVs, HHW coils, and boilers are closed.
2. Verify the system has been filled with ICW and air has been manually bled from all high point vents.
3. Line up the system to flush the desired branches through the field-installed bypasses.
4. Start the pumps and verify the system is circulating. Record the start time.
5. Periodically bleed air from the system and fill as needed with ICW.
6. Check the pressure drop on the construction strainers in the suction diffusers, and replace as needed.
7. Bleed and feed the system for a minimum of 4 hours. Southland periodically checks system pump pressures and makes sure the bleed rate does not exceed the rate of make-up.
8. Collect a system sample and verify clarity. Record the stop time.
</div> </details>
