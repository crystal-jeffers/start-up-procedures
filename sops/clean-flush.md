# Clean and Flush

Flushing, cleaning, and chemical treatment for closed loop heating hot water (HHW) systems on new construction. Each stage has its own record form you fill in on your phone.

## Purpose
This procedure covers flushing, cleaning, and chemical treatment of closed loop water systems used for heating hot water (HHW).

## Scope
This procedure applies to new construction systems.
- **EOR:** reviews and approves this plan with Southland Industries.
- **Southland:** maintains this procedure and its record forms.
- **All contractors:** comply with applicable federal, state, and local safety regulations, including lockout/tagout, hazard communication, hazardous waste handling, and electrical safety. Contractors may be asked for documented safety programs and training records.

## Materials required
- [ ] ICW (industrial cold water)
- [ ] Signs for the system valves: "System Flush in Progress"
- [ ] Replacement construction strainers and gaskets for the suction diffusers
- [ ] Cleaner: PC-1
- [ ] Corrosion inhibitor: Cori-3 or 300 (closed loop systems only)
- [ ] Total dissolved solids (TDS) test kit
- [ ] pH test kit

## The four record forms
Work through them in order. Each form has the steps for that stage, then the record.
1. [Pre-Flush](clean-flush-pre-flush): get the system ready and calculate its volume.
2. [Flushing](clean-flush-flushing): bleed and feed with ICW for at least 4 hours.
3. [Cleaning](clean-flush-cleaning): circulate cleaner, test, and flush it out.
4. [Treatment](clean-flush-treatment): charge the system with corrosion inhibitor.

## How the forms work
- **Saved as you go:** everything you type or tick is saved on this phone or computer, even if you close the page.
- **Carries over:** the System ID, description, and volume fill in on every form once you enter them.
- **Sign as you go:** ticking an activity fills in today's date and your initials. You can change either one.
- **Send it:** Save as PDF prints a clean copy. Email opens a message with every entry typed out, ready to send. Attach the PDF if one is needed.
- **Next system:** use Clear form on each form once its record is sent, so the next system starts blank.
