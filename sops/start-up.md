# Start-Up

From the last inspection to a signed turnover, in order.

## Steps

### Review and inspect
1. Review all Pre-Functional documentation.
2. Review LOTO procedures with ALL parties (including the Start-Up team).
3. Do a final visual inspection of all equipment.

### Confirm readiness
4. Coordinate power availability with the electrical contractor.
5. Verify controls will not prevent, interrupt, or delay startup (for example, no testing control valves during clean and flush).
6. Verify all waterside systems are complete and valves are open.
7. Verify all airside equipment and ducting is clean and ready to flow air.
8. Verify all zones are clean before circulating air.
9. Notify everyone that the equipment will be running.

### Power up and start
10. Turn over power from the electrical contractor to SI and apply LOTO.
11. Contact the Cx agent before any equipment startup (if required).
12. Follow all O&M procedures for startup and "bump" the loads.
13. Start systems under minimum load.
14. Gradually increase the load to maximum output, if possible.

### Run and check
15. Complete all factory, SI, and commissioning documentation.
16. Run systems and equipment in hand only while Start-Up personnel are on-site.
17. Shut the system down after a short run period and check the equipment.
18. Run systems as much as possible while on-site (burn-in).
19. Check equipment after 24, 48, and 72 hours.

### Turn over
20. Arrange a turnover meeting with all pertinent trades after startup, flush out, or clean and flush is complete.
21. Turn systems over to the next appropriate trade with signed documentation.
22. The lead reviews all documentation for the turnover package.
