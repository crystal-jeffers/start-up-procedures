# Start-Up

## Steps

<ol class="chunked-list">
<li>Review all Pre-Functional documentation.</li>
<li>Review LOTO procedures with ALL parties (including the Start-Up team).</li>
<li>Final visual inspection of all equipment.</li>
<li>Coordinate power availability with the electrical contractor.</li>
<li>Verify controls will not prevent, interrupt, or delay startup (e.g., no testing control valves during clean and flush).</li>
<li>Verify all waterside systems are complete, and valves are open.</li>
<li>Verify all airside equipment/ducting is clean and ready to flow air.</li>
<li>Verify all zones are clean before circulating air.</li>
<li>Notify everyone that the equipment will be running.</li>
<li>Turn over power from the electrical contractor to SI and apply LOTO.</li>
<li>Contact the Cx agent before any equipment startup (if required).</li>
<li>Follow all O&M procedures for startup and "bump" the loads.</li>
<li>Systems should be started under minimum load.</li>
<li>Gradually increase the load to maximum output, if possible.</li>
<li>Complete all factory, SI, and commissioning documentation.</li>
<li>Run systems and equipment in hand only while Start-Up personnel are on-site.</li>
<li>Shut the system down after a short run period and check the equipment.</li>
<li>Try to run systems as much as possible while on-site (burn-in).</li>
<li>Check equipment after 24, 48 & 72 hours.</li>
<li>Arrange a turnover meeting with all pertinent trades after completion of startup, flush out, or clean and flush.</li>
<li>Turn systems over to the next appropriate trade with signed documentation.</li>
<li>The lead must review all documentation for the turnover package.</li>
</ol>
