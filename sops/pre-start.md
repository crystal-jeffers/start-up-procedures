# Pre-Start

## Steps

1. Gather all Start-Up personnel and set expectations (safety, start & finish times, path of information, and team hierarchy).
2. Discuss short-term and long-term goals.
3. Set up personnel per task for the entire week with contingency plans (pre-task planning and workable backlog).
4. Break systems down into priorities and schedules.
5. Verify all certifications.
6. Confirm power dates are still valid.
7. Secure Clean and Flush plan (include contingency plan, riser diagram, and shop drawing on each floor with isolation valves/bypasses marked if possible).
8. Review controls SOO. Ensure you have received all updates.
9. Start Pre-Functional activities (must be completed before startup).
10. Verify there are no problems or issues that could prevent the startup process.
11. Document outstanding issues/discrepancies that may affect or prevent equipment startup.
