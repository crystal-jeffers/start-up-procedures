# Pre-Start

## Steps

<ol class="chunked-list">
<li>Gather all Start-Up personnel and set expectations (safety, start & finish times, path of information, and team hierarchy).</li>
<li>Discuss short-term and long-term goals.</li>
<li>Set up personnel per task for the entire week with contingency plans (pre-task planning and workable backlog).</li>
<li>Break systems down into priorities and schedules.</li>
<li>Verify all certifications.</li>
<li>Confirm power dates are still valid.</li>
<li>Secure Clean and Flush plan (include contingency plan, riser diagram, and shop drawing on each floor with isolation valves/bypasses marked if possible).</li>
<li>Review controls SOO. Ensure you have received all updates.</li>
<li>Start Pre-Functional activities (must be completed before startup).</li>
<li>Verify there are no problems or issues that could prevent the startup process.</li>
<li>Document outstanding issues/discrepancies that may affect or prevent equipment startup.</li>
</ol>
